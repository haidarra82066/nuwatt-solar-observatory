# Privacy and responsible publication

## Public default

Publish H3/grid or municipal aggregates, not exact residential polygons. The
public product must never contain resident names, contact details, account data,
or labels that profile household wealth or backup-power capability.

## Restricted layer

Exact source geometry, annotations, imagery references, and validation records
belong in a separately authorized workspace. Access is logged, time-bounded, and
limited to a documented research, quality-control, or operational purpose.

## Publication review

Before every release:

1. Confirm the geographic aggregation threshold.
2. Suppress sparse or sensitive cells.
3. Review critical-infrastructure overlap.
4. Confirm that source licences permit derived public aggregates.
5. Run re-identification and linkability review.
6. Verify correction, opt-out, and incident-response contacts.

Lebanese legal counsel must review the production design, including Law No.
81/2018 and any later applicable rules. This repository documents an engineering
policy and is not legal advice.
