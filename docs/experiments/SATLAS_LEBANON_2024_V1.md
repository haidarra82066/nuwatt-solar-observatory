# Lebanon Satlas screening experiment v1

## Outcome

**Disposition: share with caveats.** The experiment found 21 AI-screened
commercial-, industrial-, or utility-scale solar-farm candidates in Lebanon's
January 2024 Satlas snapshot. Two candidates independently overlap photovoltaic
generator geometries mapped in OpenStreetMap. None is promoted to panel-level
`detected` evidence because the source and review imagery are 10 m/pixel.

The public product contains 18 coarse 5 km cells. Exact source polygons remain
outside Git. No capacity or technical-yield estimate is emitted because Satlas
polygons are facility footprints, not measured module area.

These counts do not reconcile to LCEC's installed-capacity benchmark. The LCEC
figures include Lebanon's distributed rooftop market, while this experiment can
only screen installations large enough to create a multi-pixel signal in 10 m
imagery. The 21 candidates are therefore a narrow historical subset, not the
denominator of Lebanon's solar fleet.

## Sources and licences

### Satlas AI screening

The Allen Institute for AI publishes Satlas solar-farm polygons generated by
deep-learning models applied to multi-temporal Sentinel-2 imagery. The database
is ODC-BY 1.0 and permits commercial use with attribution.

Required notice: **Contains information from Satlas, made available under the
ODC Attribution License.**

The downloaded `latest_solar.shp.zip` contained the `2024-01` snapshot and had
SHA-256
`1256bf0c4a9fc58f3269f73a43ebb5f9e2a99f479e7852b1ef0cf3ae41bb7ad1`.

Satlas's published 2022-10 continental validation reports 97% solar-farm
precision and 77.9% pixel recall in Asia. Those are reference metrics—not a
97% probability for each Lebanon candidate. The source report also identifies
greenhouses and dark or metallic undeveloped land as recurring false positives.

### Sentinel-2 review imagery

Every candidate was inspected in cloud-free true-colour context from 30 March
2024 using three Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A scenes discovered through Element 84
Earth Search. Copernicus Sentinel data is free, full, and open for reproduction,
distribution, adaptation, and commercial use. The review confirms geographic
context but cannot prove panel-level identity at 10 m resolution.

### OpenStreetMap corroboration

An Overpass query returned 543 mapped Lebanon solar elements. Two Satlas
candidates had mapped photovoltaic generator geometries within 30 m: one
overlapped 27 mapped generator ways and one overlapped five. These are counted
as two corroborated candidate facilities, not 32 installations. OpenStreetMap
is ODbL 1.0; attribution is `© OpenStreetMap contributors`.

Absence from OpenStreetMap is not negative evidence because mapping is
voluntary and incomplete.

### Boundaries

Natural Earth 1:10m public-domain admin-0 and admin-1 geometry was used to
filter Lebanon and group candidates by region.

## Method

1. Download the Satlas solar-only January 2024 shapefile outside Git.
2. Intersect candidate centroids with the Natural Earth Lebanon boundary.
3. Reproject polygons to UTM zone 36N and calculate footprint area.
4. Inspect every candidate in 30 March 2024 Sentinel-2 true-colour context.
5. Query mapped solar infrastructure from OpenStreetMap and spatially match it
   within 30 m.
6. Preserve every source candidate as `screened`; add `corroborated` only when
   the independent map overlap exists.
7. Aggregate to 5 km cells and withhold exact source polygons from the public
   site.

## Data-quality assessment

The 21 Lebanon records have 21 unique identifiers and geometries, with no empty
or invalid geometry after filtering. Their footprint areas range from
1,207.639 to 10,347.062 m²; the median is 2,850.508 m². Counts and areas
reconcile exactly between source candidates, regional summaries, and the public
grid.

Material caveats:

- The January 2024 snapshot is historical and misses later installations.
- All 21 source `score` values are null, so feature-level ranking is unavailable.
- The source's `start` and `end` fields are identical across this extract and
  are not used as construction or removal dates.
- Satlas's continental metrics are not a Lebanon-specific accuracy study.
- Sentinel-2 cannot support small-rooftop completeness or module counting.
- OpenStreetMap corroboration has unknown completeness and temporal consistency.

## Publication decision

This result is appropriate as an **AI screening experiment with visible
caveats**. It is not appropriate as a production panel-detection release,
installed-capacity estimate, or replacement for LCEC's market benchmark. A
future positive rooftop release still requires licensed imagery at 0.5 m/pixel
or better, local labels, a Lebanon holdout set, calibrated thresholds, and
privacy-safe aggregation.
