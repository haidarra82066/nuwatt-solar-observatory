# Data licensing

The bundled `apps/web/public/data/demo-grid.geojson` dataset is synthetic and is
released under CC0 1.0. It exists only to exercise the platform and does not
describe measured or detected installations in Lebanon.

Future releases must include a machine-readable provenance record for every
imagery source, label source, derived feature, and aggregate. No commercial or
restricted imagery is distributed by this repository.

`apps/web/public/data/experiments/lbn-satlas-screening-2024-01-v1.geojson`
contains aggregated information derived from the Allen Institute for AI Satlas
renewable-energy product. Satlas data are made available under the Open Data
Commons Attribution License 1.0. Required attribution: **Contains information
from Satlas, made available under the ODC Attribution License.** The release
also records facility-level corroboration derived from OpenStreetMap, licensed
under ODbL, with © OpenStreetMap contributors attribution. Natural Earth
boundary data used for filtering and aggregation are public domain. These
third-party terms are not replaced by the repository's Apache-2.0 code license.

The NuWatt name and brand identity are not granted under the Apache-2.0 code
license or the CC0 demonstration-data dedication.
